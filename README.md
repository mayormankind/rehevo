# Rehevo

> **Practice the moment before it matters.**

Rehevo is a private AI rehearsal room for high-stakes communication. It is not primarily an AI feedback dashboard — it is a place to prepare, rehearse, and improve before the real moment arrives.

---

## What Rehevo Is

A focused, cinematic environment where you can practice important conversations, difficult questions, and high-pressure presentations — alone, without judgment, and with an AI that challenges you, surfaces the moments where you lost ground, and helps you get better.

**The experience follows a clear arc:**

```
PREPARE → REHEARSE → CHALLENGE → REFLECT → IMPROVE → PERFORM
```

---

## The Problem It Solves

Most people know what they *want* to say — until someone asks the question they weren't ready for. Rehevo exists to close that gap:

- **No safe way to practice.** Rehearsing in front of others is intimidating; rehearsing alone is ineffective because nothing pushes back.
- **Feedback comes too late.** By the time you've given the presentation or taken the interview, the moment has passed.
- **Preparation feels like guesswork.** Without rehearsal, readiness is a feeling, not something you can verify.

Rehevo gives you a private room to practice the pressure, not just the presentation — so you walk into the real situation knowing you've already been there.

---

## Who It's Built For

Rehevo serves people preparing for moments where communication matters:

- **Academics** — thesis defenses, research talks, grant interviews
- **Professionals** — job interviews, performance reviews, stakeholder meetings
- **Business leaders** — board presentations, investor pitches, difficult conversations
- **Anyone** facing a high-stakes communication moment who wants to be ready

---

## Core Principles

### Private
Practice badly here. Nobody else needs to see it. Rehevo is a private room by design.

### Realistic
Practice the version where someone pushes back. Rehevo's AI doesn't just listen — it challenges, interrupts, and tests your composure.

### Repeatable
The first attempt doesn't have to be good. That's why you get another one. Progress comes from practicing again, not from a single perfect run.

---

## What You Can Do

- **Prepare** — set the scene, define the context, and walk in knowing the stakes
- **Rehearse** — speak aloud in a quiet, focused room with an AI that responds in real time
- **Get challenged** — the AI asks the hard questions, interrupts when you hedge, and tests your composure
- **Reflect** — review the moments where you lost ground, not just a generic score
- **Improve** — practice specific moments again and watch yourself get better across attempts
- **Perform** — walk into the real situation with rehearsal behind you

---

## The Rehevo Experience

The product is designed around **moments**, not features:

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Stage Cue** | A small amber signal marking active attention, action, and progression |
| **Moment** | A specific point in a rehearsal worth revisiting (e.g. "04:12 — hesitated on the pricing question") |
| **Rehearsal Signal** | A controlled line representing voice, rhythm, timing, and repetition |
| **Attempt** | Progress through attempts: 01 → 02 → 03 — improvement without gamification |
| **Readiness** | Not a confidence score. Observable dimensions: Reasoning, Specificity, Clarity, Composure, Delivery |

---

## Visual Identity

Rehevo is cinematic, calm, and human — never generic SaaS, never gimmicky, never futuristic for its own sake.

- **Dark mode = rehearsal.** Focused, quiet, immersive. Feels like being in the room.
- **Light mode = reflection.** Clear, structured, readable. Feels like reviewing what happened.
- **Amber** marks the stage cue: active rehearsal, attention, action, progression.
- **Blue** carries information and analysis — never competing with amber for brand ownership.

Photography shows *the moment before*: preparation, concentration, empty stages, quiet rooms, people before they perform.
